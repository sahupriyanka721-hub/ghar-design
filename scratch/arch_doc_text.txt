15: 2. Architecture Panel — Main Objective
16: Create a dedicated portal for architects and architecture firms who want to register on Build Ghar and provide professional services to customers.
17: Architects should be able to:
18: Register using their mobile number and OTP.
19: Create an architect or architecture firm profile.
20: Enter business details and select service categories.
21: Upload professional and business verification documents.
22: Submit their application for Admin approval.
23: Access their full dashboard only after Admin approval.
24: Receive construction, renovation, architectural planning, and interior design quotation requests assigned by Admin.
25: Prepare detailed quotations and submit them to Admin.
26: Track quotations, customer decisions, bookings, and assigned projects.
27: Manage project execution, milestones, progress reports, and supporting documents.
28: Track payments, invoices, and settlements.
29: Communicate with Admin and customers through authorized platform features.
30: The complete workflow must be connected to the existing Build Ghar platform.
